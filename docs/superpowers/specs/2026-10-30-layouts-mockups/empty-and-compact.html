Porchlight · layouts mockup, part 2

The quiet and cramped states.

What each new layout does with nothing playing, in a small window of about 800 px, plus the layout dial and the Index shelf page. Same Sleeve look and real covers as the first mockup.

1Nothing playing1100 × 660

Each layout keeps its shape and shows an empty state instead of hiding its player.

Gatefold

The left page becomes an empty sleeve: "Nothing playing yet" and three picks from Home to start.

Index

The strip shows an empty cassette and reads "Nothing on the tape". Transport is dimmed.

Crate

The turntable keeps its place along the bottom with a blank empty platter, tone arm parked, nothing spinning.

Panels

The player bar is idle: empty cover slot, dimmed controls, and the right panel invites you to pick something.

2Small window800 × 600

Compact forms at about 800 px wide. Navigation, Settings and the player stay reachable in every one.

Gatefold, compact

The left page shrinks to a slim now-playing column with cover and controls. Lyrics move behind a LYRICS button. Settings is a gear next to the logo.

Index, compact

The appendix folds into a MENU button (shown open) holding playlists and Settings. The strip drops its time readout for a thin progress line.

Crate, compact

Two leaning sleeves instead of three, a smaller front cover, and a shorter turntable bar with the up-next list dropped. Settings is a gear in the header.

Panels, compact

The nav panel becomes an icon rail, and the right panel hides into the bar behind a NOW PLAYING toggle, so the cover and details are one tap away.

3The turntable dial

A small knob next to the logo with five tick marks: Sidebar, Gatefold, Index, Crate, Panels. The current layout's tick is in the accent colour.

4Index shelf page1100 × 660

Clicking a contents entry opens that Home shelf in full: "Pump it up", all 10 records, with a way back to Contents. (A couple of covers repeat because the mockup only has eight real ones.)
